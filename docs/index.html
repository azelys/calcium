The clinical frame: PTH, calcitriol, and FGF23 stabilize plasma calcium and phosphate through the gut and kidney. Bone is both a mineral reservoir and a living tissue: osteoclasts resorb matrix, osteoblasts form it, and osteocytes coordinate local adaptation.

Clinical shorthand

Terms used in the atlas

PTH
Parathyroid hormone; raises serum calcium and promotes renal phosphate loss.
Calcitriol
Active vitamin D, 1,25(OH)2D; increases intestinal mineral absorption.
FGF23
Bone-derived phosphate-regulating hormone; suppresses calcitriol and renal phosphate reabsorption.
RANKL / OPG
Coupled signals controlling osteoclast differentiation and survival.
BMU
Basic multicellular unit: a local packet of resorption followed by formation.
BSAP / CTX
Formation and resorption biomarkers used as indirect signals of bone turnover.
BMD
Bone mineral density, usually an organ-level clinical endpoint rather than a cell state.
QSP
Quantitative systems pharmacology: physiology plus disease and drug mechanisms.
